MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 29 September 2024 (the "Effective Date") by and between Orion Electronics LLC, a Nevada corporation ("Supplier"), and Zephyr Software Ltd ("Customer").
This Agreement supersedes the prior agreement between the parties dated 10 January 2024.
1. Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months.
2. Fees. Customer shall pay Supplier fees up to a total contract value of € 83.500,00, exclusive of applicable taxes.
3. Payment. Undisputed invoices are payable 2/10 net 30.
4. Renewal. Upon expiry the Agreement renews automatically for additional twelve (12) months terms. Notice of non-renewal must be served no later than 60 days before the end of the then-current term.
5. Limitation of Liability. Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed 167.000,00 EUR.
6. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Delaware.
Signed on behalf of each party on 23 September 2024.
