MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Agreement No. MSA-2025-6273
This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the 1st day of July, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Summit Consulting Inc., a California corporation ("Supplier"), and LARKSPUR LOGISTICS GMBH ("Customer").
This Agreement supersedes the prior agreement between the parties dated the 9th day of December, 2021.
1. Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the 1st day of July, 2028.
2. Fees. Customer shall pay Supplier fees up to a total contract value of $2,726,500.00, exclusive of applicable taxes.
3. Payment. Undisputed invoices are payable net 90 days from the invoice date.
4. Renewal. The parties may renew this Agreement by mutual written agreement only. Either party may terminate for convenience upon 30 days' notice.
5. Limitation of Liability. Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed USD 4,114,800.00.
6. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
Signed on behalf of each party on the 13th day of June, 2025.
