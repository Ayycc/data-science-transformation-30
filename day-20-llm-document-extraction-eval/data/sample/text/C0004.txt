PROCUREMENT AGREEMENT
This Procurement Agreement is made effective 25-Aug-2025 between Tidewater Security plc (the "Purchaser") and Highland Laboratories plc (the "Vendor").
Purchaser reference: MSA-2025-6649.
The Vendor shall supply the goods and services described in Schedule A until 25-Aug-2029.
The Purchaser's total commitment under this Agreement shall be a maximum of £1,411,900.00.
The Purchaser will settle invoices net 15 days from the invoice date.
Upon expiry the Agreement renews automatically for additional thirty-six (36) months terms. Notice of non-renewal must be served no later than 60 days before the end of the then-current term.
The Vendor's total liability is capped at £2,142,800.00.
This Agreement is subject to the laws of the State of California.
Executed on 11-Aug-2025; it replaces the letter of intent dated 28-Aug-2023.
