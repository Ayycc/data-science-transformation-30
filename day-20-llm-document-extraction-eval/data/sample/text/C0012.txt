MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Agreement No. MSA-2025-8719
This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 28 April 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Summit Medical Supply GmbH, a Nevada corporation ("Supplier"), and Ironbridge Energy LLC ("Customer").
This Agreement supersedes the prior agreement between the parties dated 12 March 2024.
1. Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 48 months.
2. Fees. Customer shall pay Supplier fees up to a total contract value of GBP 381,600.00, exclusive of applicable taxes.
3. Payment. Undisputed invoices are payable Net 60.
4. Renewal. Upon expiry the Agreement renews automatically for additional one (1) year terms. Notice of non-renewal must be served no later than 60 days before the end of the then-current term.
5. Limitation of Liability. Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed GBP 763,200.00.
6. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario.
Signed on behalf of each party on 18 April 2025.
