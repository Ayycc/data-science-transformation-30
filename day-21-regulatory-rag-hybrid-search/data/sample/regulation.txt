Model Data Protection and Conduct Regulation (SYNTHETIC)

§ 1.1 Scope
(a) This Regulation applies to every regulated entity, covered institution and licensed firm that processes personal data in the course of business.
(b) Nothing in this Regulation limits the powers of the Supervisory Authority under any other enactment.

§ 1.2 Definitions
(a) A reference to customer data includes client data and consumer data.
(b) A reference to employee data includes staff data and personnel data.
(c) A reference to vendor data includes supplier data and contractor data.
(d) A reference to payment data includes transaction data and remittance data.
(e) A reference to marketing data includes promotional data and advertising data.
(f) A reference to biometric data includes fingerprint data and facial-recognition data.
(g) A reference to location data includes geolocation data and positioning data.
(h) A reference to health data includes medical data and clinical data.
(aa) A reference to credit data includes lending data and loan data.
(ab) A reference to account data includes deposit data and savings data.
(ac) "Records" includes files and documentation.
(ad) "Complaints" includes grievances and disputes.
(ae) "Consent forms" includes authorisations and permission slips.
(af) "Transfer agreements" includes data-sharing contracts and export arrangements.
(ag) "Risk assessments" includes impact evaluations and hazard reviews.
(ah) "Access logs" includes audit trails and usage journals.
(ba) "Security incident" includes any data breach or unauthorised disclosure.

§ 2.1 Vendor complaints
(a) All supplier disputes shall be reviewed and endorsed by the board audit committee at least quarterly.
(b) The requirements of this section on contractor complaints do not apply to a covered institution that serves fewer than 250 data subjects in a calendar year.
(c) A regulated entity shall notify the Supervisory Authority of any security incident affecting vendor grievances within 30 business days of discovery of the incident.
(d) A licensed firm shall retain all vendor grievances for a period of not less than 2 years from the date of creation.
(e) A licensed firm that contravenes the requirements on supplier complaints in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $100,000 for each violation.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 2.2 Vendor access logs
(a) A regulated entity shall notify the Supervisory Authority of any security incident affecting vendor access logs within 5 calendar days of becoming aware of the event.
(b) The requirements of this section on vendor access logs do not apply to a licensed firm that serves fewer than 1,000 data subjects in a calendar year.
(c) A regulated entity that contravenes the requirements on contractor audit trails in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $40,000 for each violation.
(d) All contractor access logs shall be reviewed and approved by the Head of Internal Audit at least annually.
(e) A regulated entity shall retain all supplier usage journals for a period of not less than 12 years from the date of the last transaction.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 2.3 Vendor records
(a) A regulated entity shall notify the Supervisory Authority of any security incident affecting supplier documentation within 45 calendar days of receipt of a written request.
(b) A covered institution shall retain all vendor records for a period of not less than 7 years from the date of account closure.
(c) A regulated entity that contravenes the requirements on contractor documentation in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $5,000 for each violation.
(d) The requirements of this section on supplier records do not apply to a regulated entity that serves fewer than 1,000 data subjects in a calendar year.
(e) All supplier records shall be reviewed and endorsed by the Head of Internal Audit at least quarterly.
(f) A licensed firm shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 2.4 Vendor consent forms
(a) All vendor permission slips shall be reviewed and approved by the Chief Risk Officer at least quarterly.
(b) A licensed firm shall preserve all vendor authorisations for a period of not less than 3 years from the date of the end of the business relationship.
(c) A licensed firm that contravenes the requirements on vendor consent forms in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $20,000 for each violation.
(d) A regulated entity shall notify the Supervisory Authority of any unauthorised disclosure affecting supplier consent forms within 30 business days of discovery of the incident.
(e) The requirements of this section on supplier permission slips do not apply to a licensed firm that serves fewer than 2,500 data subjects in a calendar year.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the regulator on request.

§ 2.5 Vendor transfer agreements
(a) A licensed firm that contravenes the requirements on vendor export arrangements in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $75,000 for each violation.
(b) All vendor transfer agreements shall be reviewed and approved by the board audit committee at least annually.
(c) A regulated entity shall keep all contractor export arrangements for a period of not less than 15 years from the date of the end of the business relationship.
(d) The requirements of this section on contractor transfer agreements do not apply to a licensed firm that serves fewer than 2,500 data subjects in a calendar year.
(e) A regulated entity shall notify the Commission of any security incident affecting vendor data-sharing contracts within 14 calendar days of discovery of the incident.
(f) A covered institution shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

(c) The requirements of this section on payment transfer agreements do not apply to a regulated entity that serves fewer than 10,000 data subjects in a calendar year.
(d) A licensed firm shall keep all remittance export arrangements for a period of not less than 8 years from the date of creation.
(e) A regulated entity that breaches the requirements on transaction data-sharing contracts in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $250,000 for each violation.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 10.2 Payment records
(a) All payment files shall be reviewed and endorsed by the board audit committee at least every 24 months.
(b) A licensed firm that contravenes the requirements on payment files in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $500,000 for each violation.
(c) A regulated entity shall retain all transaction records for a period of not less than 4 years from the date of the end of the business relationship.
(d) A regulated entity shall alert the Commission of any security incident affecting remittance records within 5 calendar days of receipt of a written request.
(e) The requirements of this section on remittance documentation do not apply to a licensed firm that serves fewer than 500 data subjects in a calendar year.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the Commission on request.

§ 10.3 Payment access logs
(a) A regulated entity shall notify the regulator of any security incident affecting transaction usage journals within 45 business days of receipt of a written request.
(b) All remittance access logs shall be reviewed and approved by the Data Protection Officer at least semi-annually.
(c) The requirements of this section on payment access logs do not apply to a covered institution that serves fewer than 50 data subjects in a calendar year.
(d) A regulated entity that breaches the requirements on payment usage journals in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $250,000 for each violation.
(e) A regulated entity shall retain all payment access logs for a period of not less than 3 years from the date of account closure.
(f) A licensed firm shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 10.4 Payment risk assessments
(a) The requirements of this section on transaction risk assessments do not apply to a regulated entity that serves fewer than 5,000 data subjects in a calendar year.
(b) A licensed firm shall retain all payment impact evaluations for a period of not less than 8 years from the date of the end of the business relationship.
(c) A regulated entity that breaches the requirements on payment risk assessments in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $20,000 for each violation.
(d) A licensed firm shall alert the Supervisory Authority of any security incident affecting payment hazard reviews within 60 calendar days of the end of the reporting quarter.
(e) All transaction risk assessments shall be reviewed and approved by the Data Protection Officer at least quarterly.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 10.5 Payment complaints
(a) A covered institution shall retain all transaction complaints for a period of not less than 7 years from the date of the last transaction.
(b) All transaction disputes shall be reviewed and approved by the Chief Compliance Officer at least every 24 months.
(c) The requirements of this section on remittance disputes do not apply to a covered institution that serves fewer than 50 data subjects in a calendar year.
(d) A covered institution shall notify the regulator of any data breach affecting payment grievances within 14 calendar days of the end of the reporting quarter.
(e) A regulated entity that contravenes the requirements on remittance complaints in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 for each violation.
(f) A licensed firm shall make the policies required by this section available to the regulator on request.

§ 11.1 Marketing transfer agreements
(a) A regulated entity that breaches the requirements on advertising transfer agreements in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $20,000 for each violation.
(b) All marketing transfer agreements shall be reviewed and approved by the Head of Internal Audit at least semi-annually.
(c) The requirements of this section on promotional transfer agreements do not apply to a regulated entity that serves fewer than 1,000 data subjects in a calendar year.
(d) A regulated entity shall keep all marketing transfer agreements for a period of not less than 2 years from the date of the last transaction.
(e) A licensed firm shall alert the Commission of any security incident affecting marketing transfer agreements within 5 calendar days of the end of the reporting quarter.
(f) A licensed firm shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 11.2 Marketing consent forms
(a) A regulated entity shall inform the Supervisory Authority of any data breach affecting advertising consent forms within 90 business days of the end of the reporting quarter.
(b) All advertising permission slips shall be reviewed and endorsed by the Head of Internal Audit at least semi-annually.
(c) A regulated entity shall preserve all marketing authorisations for a period of not less than 12 years from the date of account closure.
(d) A licensed firm that contravenes the requirements on advertising permission slips in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $20,000 for each violation.
(e) The requirements of this section on promotional consent forms do not apply to a licensed firm that serves fewer than 500 data subjects in a calendar year.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the Commission on request.

§ 11.3 Marketing access logs
(a) A licensed firm that breaches the requirements on promotional access logs in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $50,000 for each violation.
(b) All marketing access logs shall be reviewed and signed off by the board audit committee at least every 24 months.
(c) A regulated entity shall retain all marketing audit trails for a period of not less than 3 years from the date of the end of the business relationship.
(d) A licensed firm shall alert the Supervisory Authority of any security incident affecting marketing audit trails within 72 business days of the end of the reporting quarter.
(e) The requirements of this section on marketing access logs do not apply to a covered institution that serves fewer than 50 data subjects in a calendar year.
(f) A regulated entity shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 11.4 Marketing risk assessments
(a) A regulated entity that violates the requirements on marketing risk assessments in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $15,000 for each violation.
(b) A licensed firm shall retain all marketing hazard reviews for a period of not less than 7 years from the date of creation.
(c) All marketing impact evaluations shall be reviewed and approved by the Chief Compliance Officer at least annually.
(d) A regulated entity shall alert the Supervisory Authority of any security incident affecting marketing hazard reviews within 3 business days of the end of the reporting quarter.
(e) The requirements of this section on marketing hazard reviews do not apply to a regulated entity that serves fewer than 5,000 data subjects in a calendar year.
(f) A licensed firm shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.

§ 11.5 Marketing records
(a) A regulated entity that contravenes the requirements on promotional documentation in this section is liable to a civil penalty of up to $150,000 for each violation.
(b) The requirements of this section on marketing records do not apply to a covered institution that serves fewer than 100 data subjects in a calendar year.
(c) All advertising files shall be reviewed and signed off by the Data Protection Officer at least every 24 months.
(d) A covered institution shall retain all advertising documentation for a period of not less than 15 years from the date of the last transaction.
(e) A regulated entity shall notify the regulator of any unauthorised disclosure affecting advertising records within 20 calendar days of the end of the reporting quarter.
(f) A licensed firm shall make the policies required by this section available to the Supervisory Authority on request.
